The apps below all track time and add timers. What makes FocusForge different is operating at the level of psychology, not just metrics. Each feature here does something no competitor does today.

Feature 01 — Pattern Intelligence
Doom Loop Detector

Detect anxiety-scrolling, not just screen time

Current apps count minutes. But opening Instagram 9 times in 15 minutes and leaving after 20 seconds each time is fundamentally different behavior from intentionally watching 3 videos. One is a compulsion loop. The other is leisure. Nobody distinguishes between them.

How it works: Track open-count per app per 15-minute window. If opens > 5 with avg session < 45s → trigger a mindful pause card: "You've opened Instagram 9 times in 12 minutes. You seem restless. What are you actually looking for?" → [Bored] [Stressed] [Procrastinating] [Just checking]. These self-tags build a personal trigger map over time.

Feature 02 — Paradigm Shift
Earned Time Bank

Leisure as reward, not guilt

Every screen time app frames phone use as the enemy. This creates a shame loop: users feel bad → open app to feel better → feel worse → abandon app. FocusForge flips the frame: focused time EARNS guilt-free leisure time. You're not fighting your phone. You're unlocking it.

How it works: Each focus session deposits minutes into a Time Bank. 25-min session = 15 min banked leisure. Bank resets weekly. Dashboard shows: "You've earned 1h 40m of guilt-free scroll time this week. Spend it however you want." Transforms the psychology from restriction → reward.

Feature 03 — Mindful Friction
The 5-Second Pause

Interrupt the reflex, not the person

Hard blocks get overridden. They create frustration, not self-awareness. The 5-second pause creates a moment of conscious choice — enough to break the auto-pilot loop without feeling punished.

How it works: When app is "focused," tapping a tracked app shows a beautiful 5-second countdown with one question: "What are you actually looking for?" → [Something specific, let me in] [Just a habit, keep blocking]. No shame, full choice. Self-awareness compounds over weeks.

Feature 04 — Emotional Intelligence
The Mirror

Your emotional trigger map

People doom scroll for a reason. Stress, boredom, avoidance, loneliness. Understanding your specific triggers is 10x more valuable than knowing you used TikTok for 47 minutes.

How it works: One-tap mood check (5 emoji) before and after sessions. After 2 weeks: "You tend to reach for Instagram when you're anxious — usually Sunday evenings after 8pm. Breaking that link earned you 3h this week."

Feature 05 — Living Visual
Digital Garden

Your habits as a living world

Numbers don't create emotional investment. A garden you've been growing for 3 weeks does. Visualizing your consistency as something alive and beautiful creates loss aversion that actually works — you don't want to let your garden wilt.

How it works: Each focus session plants a flower. 7-day streak grows a tree. Missing a day wilts today's flowers (not the whole garden — no nuclear resets). Beautiful, shareable, emotionally sticky. The viral loop is people sharing their garden progress.

Feature 06 — Predictive AI
Vulnerability Score

Pre-empt doom scrolling before it starts

Pattern data reveals when you're most likely to fall into compulsive usage. Acting before the spiral starts is 10x easier than stopping it mid-scroll.

How it works: After 2 weeks, calculates risk based on: time of day + day of week + mood check-ins. "High risk window: Sunday 9pm is your #1 doom scroll time. Pre-lock your phone?" → One tap to start a protective session.

Bonus — Virality Mechanic
Time Translator

Reframe time lost in human terms, not minutes

"You spent 2h 14m on social media" means nothing to the brain. The same time translated into lived equivalents creates a visceral reaction: it becomes the shareable, emotional hook that drives organic growth.

How it works: Usage stats are automatically translated — "This week's social media time equals: 1 novel chapter · a full workout · learning 20 Spanish words · 4 long phone calls with friends." Tap to share as a story card. This is how BePresent got 151M TikTok views — the "oh wow" moment of seeing time in real terms. FocusForge makes it native, automatic, and beautiful.
